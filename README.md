### 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a software and backend developer with a **B.Sc. in Electronic Engineering** and experience spanning **business applications, backend development, IT operations, and embedded systems**. I like solving challenging problems and taking ownership of a solution beyond the code: architecture, integration, deployment, security, and ongoing maintenance.

In my current role as **IT Lead / Software & Backend Developer at Semlinger & Gruppe** (since March 2025), my work combines custom applications and WordPress/WooCommerce plugins with Linux-based hosting, Docker services, Microsoft administration, and business process automation.

My current interests include **AI automation**, locally hosted language models, intelligent assistants, and connecting AI capabilities to real-world workflows.
